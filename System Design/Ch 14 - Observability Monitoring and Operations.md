# Chapter 14: Observability, Monitoring, and Operations

## Observability

Observability is the ability to understand the internal state of a system based on its external outputs. It is used to ensure the reliability, availability, and performance of the system.

### Monitoring

Monitoring is the process of collecting and analyzing data about the performance and health of a system. It is used to ensure the reliability, availability, and performance of the system.

### Logging

Logging is the process of recording events and activities in a system. It is used to ensure the reliability, availability, and performance of the system.

### Metrics

### Tracing

Tracing is the process of tracking the flow of requests through a system. It is used to ensure the reliability, availability, and performance of the system.

## Structured Logging

Structured logging is the process of recording events and activities in a system in a structured format. It is used to ensure the reliability, availability, and performance of the system.

### Centralized Logging

### Correlation IDs

### Distributed Tracing
